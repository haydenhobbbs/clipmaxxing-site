Privacy Policy

Clip Maxxing Uploader · Last updated November 24, 2026

Clip Maxxing Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Clip Maxxing YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what it does with it.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies to data handled through Google and YouTube.

What data the app accesses

  • An OAuth sign-in token for the channel owner's Google account, used to upload videos to that channel.
  • Basic information about the owner's own channel (name, handle) and statistics for videos on that channel (for example views and watch time), used to see which clips perform well.

The app does not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube users, viewers or commenters.

How the data is used and stored

  • The sign-in token and channel statistics are stored only on the channel owner's own computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Video files and their titles and descriptions are sent to YouTube only when the owner approves an upload.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Locally stored tokens and statistics can be deleted by the owner at any time; stored statistics are refreshed or deleted within 30 days.

Cookies

The app does not place cookies or similar technologies on any user's browser or device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
